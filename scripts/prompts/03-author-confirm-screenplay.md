# Confirm the canonical screenplay

Run:

```bash
msb inspect <folder> --screenplay
```

Read the rendered screenplay text — never the JSON — and confirm it says what your draft meant: every line present and verbatim, speakers right, the pacing and total duration acceptable as creative decisions of yours. Words and timing are both yours to own.

If anything is missing, mis-attributed, or paced wrong, send it back to the Producer's canonicalization step naming the exact cues at fault. Approve only when the rendered screenplay is faithful to the draft.
