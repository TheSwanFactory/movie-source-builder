# Review the animatic

Run:

```bash
msb animatic <folder>
```

Watch `cuts/animatic.mp4` — the zero-cost review movie assembled from the timed cues and the boards, before any shot list exists. Check whether cast, setting, beat order, and pacing match intent; the cue timing you confirmed in the screenplay is what you are watching play out.

Flag any stretch whose board or pacing needs revision before anything paid happens — send it back to the board step, or further back to the canonical screenplay (a re-pacing moves cue times but never cue ids, so board anchors survive). The animatic is deterministic and regenerable; rerun it after every revision.
