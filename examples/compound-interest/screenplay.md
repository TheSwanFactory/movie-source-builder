# The Marshmallow Investment

## Scene 1: One Marshmallow

**Setting:** A cheerful cardboard bank set. Sockrates is a blue sock puppet with round button eyes. Penny is an orange sock puppet with green yarn hair.

**Sockrates:** Penny, this marshmallow is my entire retirement fund.

**Penny:** Then your retirement looks delicious—and dangerously unsecured.

## Scene 2: Let It Grow

**Sockrates:** The bank says it earns ten percent a year. What does that mean?

**Penny:** First you earn a little extra. Then the extra starts earning extra. That is compound interest.

## Scene 3: The Temptation

**Sockrates:** So if I leave it alone, one marshmallow can become many?

**Penny:** Exactly. Your hardest financial decision is not eating the principal.

**Sockrates:** I suddenly understand both investing and sacrifice.
